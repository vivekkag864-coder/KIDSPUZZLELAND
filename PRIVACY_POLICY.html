Privacy Policy for Kids Puzzle Land

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Kids Puzzle Land (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects the privacy and safety of children, parents, and families. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, when we share it, and what choices parents and guardians have.

This policy is intended to support responsible digital practices for child-focused apps and services. We collect only what is reasonably necessary to provide a safe, useful, and enjoyable experience.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, parental contact details, profile name, and account recovery information if provided.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system, app version, language preferences, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, crash reports, and diagnostic data.
  • Gameplay and app usage data: puzzle progression, scores, achievements, level completion, play time, feature usage, preferences, and learning activity data.
  • Support and communication data: messages sent to us, support tickets, feedback, surveys, and service requests.
  • Payment information: if a parent purchases a premium or subscription feature, payment details are processed through a secure third-party payment provider. We do not store full payment card information on our servers.

We do not sell personal information.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the app and gameplay experience.
  • Personalize learning difficulty, recommendations, and user experience.
  • Manage accounts, login sessions, and account recovery.
  • Support customer service, troubleshooting, and bug resolution.
  • Detect fraud, misuse, abuse, or technical issues.
  • Measure performance, app stability, and feature engagement.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Children’s Privacy

We recognize that Kids Puzzle Land is designed for children and families. We aim to limit collection and use to what is necessary for the service to operate properly and safely.

  • We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.
  • We encourage parents and guardians to review account settings and manage their child’s use of the app.
  • We do not use personal information to target children with behavioral advertising.
  • We do not knowingly sell personal data or share it for marketing purposes.
Important: Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising their child’s use of the app and managing any account or device settings related to advertising, privacy, and parental controls.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services to support operations, including cloud hosting, analytics, customer support, payments, and advertising. One such advertising service is Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect and use information such as:

  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers
  • App usage data and ad interaction data
  • Limited device and technical information

AdMob may use this information to display ads, measure ad performance, improve ad relevance, and prevent fraud or misuse, in accordance with its own privacy policy and applicable platform guidelines.

We do not knowingly serve personalized or behavioral advertising to children in a manner that violates applicable law, child-directed platform rules, or family privacy expectations. When required by law or platform policies, we may limit ad personalization and use only contextual or non-personalized advertising for child-directed experiences.

Parents may manage advertising preferences on their device and in the settings of the relevant operating system or Google account.

5. Cookies, Local Storage, and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to:

  • Remember user preferences and settings
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Understand feature usage and troubleshoot issues
  • Reduce repeated prompts or login friction

Parents can manage browser settings to disable cookies, but some website or app features may not function properly if cookies or local storage are disabled.

6. Data Sharing

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With the parent or guardian of the account holder
  • With trusted service providers that help us operate the app
  • To comply with applicable law, subpoena, court order, or legal process
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of users or the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets, where the receiving entity agrees to honor this Privacy Policy

We do not share personal information for direct marketing or unrelated third-party advertising purposes.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to operate the service, maintain accounts, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

When information is no longer needed, we delete, anonymize, or securely de-identify it when reasonably possible.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Parents and guardians may:

  • Review or update account information
  • Request deletion of a child account and associated data
  • Limit or disable optional communications
  • Ask questions about our data practices or privacy settings

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address listed below.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We encourage parents and users to protect account credentials and notify us immediately if they suspect unauthorized access or misuse.

10. International Data Transfers

If information is transferred outside your country, we will take appropriate steps to ensure it is handled in a manner consistent with applicable privacy laws and reasonable security standards.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of the policy. We encourage parents and guardians to review this policy periodically.

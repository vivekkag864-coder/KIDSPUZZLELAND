# Privacy Policy for Kids Puzzle Land

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Kids Puzzle Land (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy and safety of children and families who use our app and website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, and the choices available to parents and guardians.

This policy is designed to align with the privacy expectations of families and to support responsible data practices for children’s services.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

- Account information: name, email address, parent/guardian contact details, and profile information provided during sign-up or account recovery.
- Device and usage information: device type, operating system, app version, language preferences, crash reports, and technical diagnostics.
- Gameplay data: puzzle progress, scores, achievements, level completion, time spent in app, and content preferences.
- Support data: messages sent to us, survey responses, and requests for help.
- Payment information: only if a parent purchases a premium feature or subscription. Payment details are processed by a secure third-party payment provider and are not stored by us directly.

We do not sell personal information.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the app and gameplay experience.
- Personalize puzzle difficulty, recommendations, and learning activities.
- Support customer service and account access.
- Detect and prevent fraud, misuse, abuse, or technical issues.
- Maintain security and monitor performance.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

## 3. Children’s Privacy

We recognize that Kids Puzzle Land is intended for children and families. We limit collection to what is necessary to operate the service and provide a safe experience.

- We do not knowingly ask children to provide unnecessary personal information.
- We encourage parents/guardians to review settings and manage their child’s account.
- We do not use personal information to target children with behavioral advertising.
- We do not knowingly sell or share personal information for marketing purposes.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

We may use trusted third-party services to support operations, including:

- analytics and app performance tools,
- cloud hosting and storage providers,
- customer support tools,
- payment processors,
- advertising providers such as Google AdMob.

The Kids Puzzle Land app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app usage information, and limited device information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. AdMob may also use this information in accordance with its own Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising requirements.

We do not knowingly serve personalized or behavioral advertising to children in a manner that violates applicable law or platform policies. Where required by law or platform requirements, we may limit ad personalization and use only contextual or non-personalized advertising for child-directed experiences. Parents can manage ad settings on their device and in the Google settings of the device used to access the app.

These providers may process information only as needed to perform their services and are expected to protect it under appropriate safeguards.

## 5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to:

- remember user preferences,
- improve app performance,
- understand how features are used,
- avoid repeated prompts.

Parents can manage browser settings to disable cookies; however, some features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

## 6. Data Sharing

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

- with a parent/guardian or authorized account holder,
- with service providers that help us operate the app,
- to comply with applicable law, court order, or legal process,
- to protect the safety, rights, or property of users or the public,
- in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets, where the receiving entity agrees to honor this Privacy Policy.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed to provide services, maintain accounts, satisfy legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it where possible.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

Parents and guardians may:

- review or update account information,
- request deletion of a child account and associated personal data,
- withdraw consent for optional communications,
- contact us to ask questions about this policy.

To exercise these rights, please email us at the contact address listed below.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no system is completely secure. We encourage users to protect their account credentials and notify us immediately if they suspect unauthorized access.

## 10. International Transfers

If information is transferred outside your home country, we will take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws and appropriate safeguards.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of the page. We encourage parents and guardians to review this policy periodically.
